Why convert WebP to JPG?

While WebP is great for web speed, JPG remains the king of compatibility for several reasons:

  • Universal Support: JPG files can be opened by virtually any software, app, or operating system.
  • Print Ready: Most printing services and home printers handle JPG files better than WebP.
  • Easy Editing: Legacy photo editing tools may not support WebP, but they all support JPG.
  • Social Media: Some platforms may still have issues displaying certain WebP encodings.
