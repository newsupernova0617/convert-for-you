Why Use PNG for your PDF Pages?

When image quality is the top priority, PNG is the best choice over JPG. Here is why:

  • Lossless Quality: No compression artifacts. Every pixel from the original PDF is preserved.
  • Transparency: If your PDF has a transparent background, PNG will keep it.
  • Design Material: Ideal for importing document snippets into Photoshop, Illustrator, or Canva.
  • Secure & Fast: Desktop-level speed directly in your browser with 256-bit SSL encryption.
