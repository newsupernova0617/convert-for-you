Why use JPG instead of PNG?

While PNG is high-quality, JPG (Joint Photographic Experts Group) is often the better choice for certain scenarios:

  • Smaller File Size: JPG uses lossy compression, which can reduce file size by up to 10x compared to PNG.
  • Ideal for Photos: Complex images with millions of colors look great in JPG format.
  • Faster Loading: Smaller JPG files mean your website pages load much faster for visitors.
  • Universal compatibility: Every single image viewer in the world can open a JPG file.
