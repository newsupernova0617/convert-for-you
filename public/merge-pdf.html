Why Should You Merge Your PDF Files?

Cluttering your inbox with multiple attachments is inefficient. PDF Merging allows for:

  • Unified Submissions: Combine resumes, portfolios, and letters into one professional document.
  • Organized Storage: Keep all related receipts or monthly reports in a single file for easy retrieval.
  • Easier Sharing: Send one file instead of ten, reducing confusion for the recipient.
