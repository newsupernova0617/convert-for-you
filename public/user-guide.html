User Guide

Learn how to make the most of Convert4U's powerful tools

1. Working with PDF Tools

Our PDF suite is designed for ultimate flexibility. Whether you're merging or compressing, the workflow is consistent:

1
Select Your Files

Click the upload box or drag your PDF documents directly into the browser. You can select multiple files for tools like 'Merge PDF'.

2
Configure Settings

For 'Split PDF', indicate the page ranges. For 'Compress PDF', the engine automatically optimizes balances between quality and size.

3
Download Instantly

Once processing reaches 100%, click the download button to save your new file. Files are deleted after 1 hour for your security.

2. Image Magic: Resizing & Converting

Optimize your visuals for web or print with our image processing tools:

  • JPG/PNG Conversion: Switch between formats while maintaining transparency or maximizing compression.
  • HEIC to JPG: Easily turn your iPhone photos into universal formats compatible with any device.
  • Image Resizing: Enter your target width, and our tool will handle the height to keep the aspect ratio perfect.

3. Pro Tips for Faster Conversions

Fast Uploads

Smaller files process faster. Use 'Compress' before 'Merge' if you're handling massive documents.

Security First

We use 256-bit SSL. Always download your files within 60 minutes before they are wiped.
