Why PNG is better for Design?

While HEIC saves space, PNG is the king of web and design compatibility. By converting, you get:

  • Transparency Support: PNG preserves the alpha channel (transparent backgrounds) which is essential for logos and web assets.
  • Lossless Quality: Unlike JPEG, PNG uses lossless compression, ensuring every pixel remains sharp.
  • Universal Support: PNG can be viewed in all web browsers, Photoshop, and social media platforms without plugins.
  • Easy Sharing: Send your photos to Windows and Android users without worrying about 'unsupported format' errors.
