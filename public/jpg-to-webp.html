Why use WebP instead of JPG?

Developed by Google, WebP is a modern image format that provides superior lossless and lossy compression for images on the web:

  • Better Compression: WebP lossy images are 25-34% smaller than comparable JPEG images.
  • Improved SEO: Faster images lead to faster page loads, which is a key ranking factor for Google.
  • Maintain Quality: WebP achieves smaller file sizes without sacrificing visual fidelity.
  • Modern Browser Support: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge all support WebP natively.
