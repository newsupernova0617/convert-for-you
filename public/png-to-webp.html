WebP: The Best format for PNG users

Standard PNG files can be quite heavy. WebP provides the best alternative for transparent images:

  • Retain Transparency: Just like PNG, WebP supports transparent backgrounds, making it ideal for logos.
  • Significant Compression: WebP files are typically 26% smaller in size compared to PNGs.
  • Lossless & Lossy: You can choose the right balance between quality and performance.
  • Google Favored: Using WebP can help improve your site's PageSpeed Insights score.
