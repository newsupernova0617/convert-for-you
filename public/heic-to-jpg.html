What is HEIC and Why Convert?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is Apple's primary photo format. While it saves space, it is notorious for being hard to open on non-Apple devices:

  • Compatibility: Windows, Android, and many websites still don't support HEIC natively.
  • Universal Sharing: JPG is accepted everywhere, making it the safest format for sharing.
  • No Software Needed: You don't need to install Apple's extensions on Windows. Just use our tool!
  • MetaData: Our converter attempts to preserve your photo's original EXIF data (date, settings, etc.).
