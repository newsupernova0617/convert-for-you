Why Use PDF for Your Presentations?

PowerPoint is best for editing, but PDF is superior for official presenting and sharing:

  • No Font Substitutions: Your custom fonts will never be broken on someone else's screen.
  • Fixed Layout: Objects and images don't move or overlap unexpectedly between different software versions.
  • Universal Viewing: Send your deck to clients knowing they can open it on any device without Microsoft Office.
  • Fast & Secure: Files are protected by SSL and permanently purged from our servers within 1 hour.
